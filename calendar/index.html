Link your tournament calendar

For the Android app’s Settings → Tournament calendar: this page turns your tournament calendar’s link into a QR code for the app to scan, so you don’t have to send the link to your phone.

Every event in the calendar you link shows as a tournament. So keep your tournaments in a calendar of their own. You set it up once, then add each tournament there. It has its own link, so linking it shares nothing else.

  1. Make a tournament calendar (once), and give it a color of its own, so you remember it’s where tournaments go:
    • Google Calendar: on a computer, ＋ beside Other calendars → Create new calendar → name it, for example “Pool tournaments” → Create calendar. (The phone app can’t make one.)
    • Outlook: at outlook.com, in Calendar: Add calendar → Create blank calendar → name it → Save.
    • Apple (iCloud): in the Calendar app on an iPhone or iPad: Calendars → Add Calendar → name it, in your iCloud account → Done.
  2. Copy your tournament calendar’s link:
    • Google Calendar: Settings → your tournament calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.
    • Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar (your tournament calendar, Can view titles and locations) → Publish → the ICS link.
    • Apple (iCloud): at icloud.com/calendar, the share button beside your tournament calendar → Public Calendar → Copy Link.
  3. Paste it here and tap Make the QR code.
  4. In the app: Settings → Tournament calendar → Scan a QR code, and point the phone at the code.

The link is a secret: anyone with it can read that calendar. This page works entirely in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere (it isn’t even counted as a visit), keeps nothing, and clears the code when you tap Clear, after 5 minutes, or when you close the page. Copying the link puts it on your clipboard. If your clipboard syncs to other devices (Windows: Settings → System → Clipboard), turn that off before you copy it, and delete the link from your clipboard history (Windows+V) when you’re done. If the link has already gone somewhere it shouldn’t, make a new one (Google: Reset in the same settings; Outlook: Reset links; Apple: turn Public Calendar off, which stops the link working; if turning it back on gives the same link, move your events to a new calendar) and bring it over the same way, with sync off.
