Link your tournament calendar

For the Android app’s Settings → Tournament calendar: this page turns your Google Calendar link into a QR code for the app to scan, so you don’t have to send the link to your phone.

  1. In Google Calendar on this computer: Settings → your pool calendar → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.
  2. Paste it here and tap Make the QR code.
  3. In the app: Settings → Tournament calendar → Scan a QR code, and point the phone at the code.

The link is a secret: anyone with it can read that calendar. This page works entirely in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere (it isn’t even counted as a visit), keeps nothing, and clears the code when you tap Clear, after 5 minutes, or when you close the page. Copying the link puts it on your clipboard. If your clipboard syncs to other devices (Windows: Settings → System → Clipboard), turn that off before you copy it, and delete the link from your clipboard history (Windows+V) when you’re done. If the link has already gone somewhere it shouldn’t, Reset it in the same Google Calendar settings and bring the new one over the same way, with sync off.
